# 12-Axis Camless CNC Spring & 3D Wire Former — Bangalore & Hosur Setup Guide

An interactive, engineer-grade single-page application and decision radar for setting up and operating a **12-Axis Camless CNC Spring & Wire Forming Machine** (wire diameters **Ø 0.5 mm to 4.0 mm**, capital cost **₹28 Lakhs to ₹44 Lakhs**) in the Greater Bangalore and Hosur industrial manufacturing corridors.

🔗 **Live Web Application:** [https://shaikmdnisaar.github.io/12-axis-camless-former/](https://shaikmdnisaar.github.io/12-axis-camless-former/)

## 🎯 Key Modules & Features

### 1. Interactive 12-Axis Radial Head Visualizer
* **SVG Kinematic Schematic:** 8 independent radial servo slide arms (S1 to S8 at 45° intervals), central rotating quill with dual feed rollers, and dual motorized auxiliary servo spinners (SP1 & SP2).
* **Click-to-Inspect Inspector:** Real-time display of AC servo ratings (1.5 kW – 4.5 kW), tooling attachments (carbide coiling pins, guillotine shear blade, deflection rollers), and CNC cycle functions.

### 2. Machine Models Benchmark (₹28L – ₹44L Landed)
* **Dongguan YLSK-1240:** Ø 1.2 – 4.0 mm, Baoyuan/Advantech IPC, max 110 m/min feed rate, automotive suspension & seat frame specialist (~₹32.5L – ₹38.0L).
* **Solarman CMM-12-236R:** Ø 0.8 – 3.6 / 4.0 mm, Kaifu/Qianye CNC, high-speed extension & double-torsion springs (~₹28.0L – ₹34.5L).
* **CNC-1280 / WNJ-1280 Heavy Duty:** High-rigidity box structure for high-tensile wire forms, exhaust brackets, and agricultural tines (~₹37.0L – ₹44.0L).

### 3. Product Scope (0.5 mm to 4.0 mm)
* **Compression Springs:** Conical, barrel, hourglass, variable pitch (80–160 pcs/min).
* **Extension Springs:** English loop, German crossover hook without secondary looping machines (40–85 pcs/min).
* **Double-Torsion Springs:** Central bridge with asymmetric legs (35–70 pcs/min).
* **3D Spatial Wire Forms:** Non-planar multi-axis bends enabled by quill rotation without twisting the wire spool.

### 4. Critical Business Factors & Decision Radar
* **Working Capital Mismatch:** 60–90 day OEM credit cycles (Honda Narasapura, TVS Hosur, Bosch) vs advance steel wire payments; liquid reserve requirement (₹12L–₹18L).
* **Scrap Rate Control:** Keeping scrap strictly < 1.2% via Camless computerized program recall vs 5–8% on mechanical cam machines.
* **Automotive PPAP Level 3:** $C_{pk} > 1.67$ capability, load-deflection hysteresis testing, 720h salt spray resistance.
* **Continuous In-Line Stress Relieving:** 250°C–380°C mesh-belt conveyor oven at discharge chute.
* **Carbide Tooling Maintenance:** Sub-micron YG15/YG20 tungsten carbide tooling maintenance in Peenya 3rd Phase.
* **Clean Power Conditioning:** 35–40 kVA copper-wound servo stabilizer + 5 kVA online isolation UPS.

### 5. Bangalore & Surrounding Industrial Clusters Matrix
Detailed side-by-side comparison of shed rentals, power infrastructure, and logistics across:
* **Peenya Industrial Area (Stages 1–4):** The tooling & heat-treatment heart of Bangalore.
* **Narasapura Industrial Area (Kolar / NH 75):** Direct Tier-1 access to Honda Motorcycle & Scooter India (HMSI) mega-plant and fastest container haulage from Chennai Sea Port.
* **Bommasandra & Jigani (South Bangalore):** Proximity to Bosch, TVS, and Toyota supply chains.
* **Dabaspet & Nelamangala (Tumkur Rd):** Lowest rental costs (₹14–₹20/sq.ft) and spacious crane-equipped sheds.
* **Hosur SIPCOT (TN Border - 35km):** Lower TANGEDCO power tariffs and two-wheeler OEM ecosystem.
* **Bidadi (Mysore Road):** Toyota Kirloskar Motor supplier corridor.

### 6. Interactive CapEx & OpEx ROI Calculator
* Reactive sliders for machine cost (₹28L–₹44L), monthly wire tonnage (1–12 tons), raw material cost (₹85–₹220/kg), and selling price (₹160–₹420/kg).
* Live computation of gross margins, monthly net profit after rent/BESCOM/labor, and payback break-even periods.

### 7. Animated Visual 90-Day Factory Setup Roadmap
* Interactive 5-phase visual stepper with animated SVG diagrams:
  1. *Phase 1 (Days 1–20):* Contract, LC Advance & Shed Lease
  2. *Phase 2 (Days 21–45):* BESCOM LT-5 415V Power, M25 Flooring & 7.5 Bar Compressed Air
  3. *Phase 3 (Days 46–65):* Ocean Transit, Chennai Port Customs (HSN 846229) & NH 75 Haulage
  4. *Phase 4 (Days 66–75):* 5-Ton Hydra Crane Rigging, Spirit Leveling (<0.02 mm/m) & 12-Axis Calibration
  5. *Phase 5 (Days 76–90):* Wire Trials, Mesh Belt Tempering Oven & Automotive PPAP Approval
* Auto-Tour mode with automated cycling every 4.5 seconds.
