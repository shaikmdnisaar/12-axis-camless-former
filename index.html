Zero Cam Setup Time

Traditional cam formers require 4 to 8 hours to grind, position, and tune physical cam lobes. The 12-axis camless machine setups take only 25 to 45 minutes via computerized MPG teach mode and program recall.

📐

Simultaneous 3D Kinematics

Each radial slide operates independently with dedicated ball screws. Free synchronization allows extreme geometric freedom: asymmetric double-torsion legs, intricate English & German hooks, and automotive seat wires.

🎯

Probe Closed-Loop QC

High-speed touch probes verify initial bend angles. If springback occurs (common with batch-to-batch wire tensile variation in India), the CNC automatically offsets the servo arm angle in real-time.

INVESTOR & PROMOTER DECISION MATRIX

Critical Factors to Consider for This Business

Spring and wire-forming offers high gross margins (30%–45%), but profitability in the Bangalore-Hosur industrial cluster hinges on managing these six fundamental financial, metallurgical, and market dynamics:

💰 CRITICAL RISK

Working Capital Mismatch (Credit Cycles)

The Reality: Steel wire manufacturers (Tata Steel, Bansal, Usha Martin) or Peenya stockists demand advance payment or maximum 15-day credit. Meanwhile, automotive OEMs (Honda Narasapura, TVS Hosur, Bosch) operate on strict 60 to 90 days credit terms.

Key Mitigation Strategy Maintain a dedicated liquid working capital buffer of ₹12L – ₹18L (or obtain a bank Cash Credit / CC limit) exclusively for 2–3 months of raw material inventory and monthly overheads.
📉 PROFIT SENSITIVE

Raw Material Scrap Rate (55–65% Cost Share)

The Reality: High-tensile spring steel wire accounts for nearly 60% of total unit manufacturing cost. On mechanical cam machines, trial-and-error setup wastes 50–150 test coils per batch. If scrap hits 4–6%, entire net profit is erased.

Key Mitigation Strategy The 12-axis camless former computerizes program recall and MPG teach-in mode, locking dimensional tolerances in < 5 trial pieces. Keep total plant scrap strictly below 1.2%.
📋 MARKET ENTRY BARRIER

Automotive PPAP Level 3 & Quality Compliance

The Reality: Tier-1 auto OEMs will not accept delivery without Production Part Approval Process (PPAP). They audit process capability (CPK > 1.67), load-deflection hysteresis curves, and 720-hour salt spray corrosion testing.

Key Mitigation Strategy Budget upfront for an in-house Digital Dual-Load Cell Spring Tester (compression & extension) and a 2D Optical Profile Projector (VMS) to self-certify inspection reports before customer audits.
🔥 METALLURGICAL NECESSITY

Residual Stress Relieving (Tempering Dwell)

The Reality: Cold-drawn wire undergoes massive localized dislocation during rapid coiling. Unrelieved springs will suffer permanent set (loss of free length) within 5,000 load cycles in vehicle service, leading to OEM rejections and line-stoppage penalties.

Key Mitigation Strategy Integrate a continuous mesh-belt electric conveyor furnace (250°C–380°C) directly under the former discharge chute for zero-handling, instantaneous stress-relief tempering.
🛠️ OPERATIONAL VITAL

Carbide Tooling Maintenance & Spares

The Reality: Hardened music wire (SWPB) and oil-tempered silicon chrome wire (OT-SiCr) cause abrasive wear on feed roller V/U grooves, quill lips, and cutoff blades. Worn wire feed rollers slip, producing inconsistent spring free lengths.

Key Mitigation Strategy Use only Sub-Micron Tungsten Carbide (YG15 / YG20 grade) tooling. Establish rapid-regrind contracts with precision toolrooms in Peenya 3rd Phase to re-lap cutters and quills within 24 hours.
⚡ INFRASTRUCTURE VITAL

Grid Power Quality & Voltage Swings

The Reality: Bangalore industrial corridors (Peenya, Narasapura, Bommasandra) experience seasonal grid surges and brownouts (360V to 465V). Voltage spikes can blow expensive Japanese Yaskawa AC servo drive amplifier boards.

Key Mitigation Strategy Mandatory installation of a 35–40 kVA Copper-wound Balanced Servo Voltage Stabilizer + an dedicated 5 kVA Online Isolation UPS for the CNC Industrial PC controller.
PHYSICAL PLANT & ELECTRICAL INFRASTRUCTURE

Factory Layout & Bangalore Utility Requirements

Everything required inside your factory shed before the container arrives at Bangalore ICD or your facility gate.

⚡

BESCOM Industrial Power Connection

  • Sanctioned Load: Minimum 25 HP (18.6 kW), recommended 35 HP (26 kW) to power the former + continuous tempering oven + compressor together.
  • BESCOM Tariff Category: LT-5 (Industrial Power). Commercial tariff ~₹7.20 to ₹8.10 per kWh depending on slab and time-of-day (TOD) metering.
  • Servo Stabilizer: 35 – 40 kVA Copper-wound, three-phase oil/air-cooled stabilizer (415V ± 1%). Essential in Bangalore industrial feeders prone to voltage fluctuations (360V - 460V spikes).
  • Online UPS for CNC: 5 kVA online UPS with 15-minute battery bank dedicated exclusively to CNC controller IPC, preventing drive fault code corruption during power switchovers.
💨

Pneumatics & Compressed Air System

  • Air Compressor: 5.0 HP or 7.5 HP Rotary Screw Air Compressor (e.g., Elgi Equipments, Chicago Pneumatic, or Atlas Copco available in Peenya).
  • Pressure Rating: 7.0 to 8.5 Bar constant working pressure for pneumatic wire clamps, slide boosters, and auto cutoffs.
  • Air Treatment: Refrigerated Air Dryer + 3-Stage Pre/Post Filters (0.01 micron particle & oil removal). Oil/moisture in air will ruin sensitive pneumatic solenoid valves.
  • Air Receiver Tank: 300 to 500 Liters vertical MS receiver tank with auto-drain valve.
🏗️

Floor Loading & Shed Dimensions

  • Minimum Shed Area: 1,200 to 1,800 sq. ft. clear floor space (allows machine, decoiler clearance, tempering furnace line, inspection lab, and raw material coil storage).
  • Flooring Spec: 150 mm to 200 mm reinforced concrete (M25 grade) with anti-dust epoxy coating. Machine weight is approx 4,500 – 6,200 kg.
  • Vibration Dampening: Heavy-duty leveling anti-vibration mount pads (supplied with machine or Dynemech make from Bangalore).
  • Ceiling Height: Minimum 12 – 14 ft. clearance for loading 1-ton wire coils onto the vertical uncoiler turntable.
🔥

Auxiliary Post-Processing (Stress Relieving)

  • Stress Relieving Requirement: Cold-formed spring steel wire contains high residual stresses. Immediately after coiling, springs must undergo stress relieving at 250°C to 380°C for 20-30 mins.
  • Continuous Conveyor Oven: In-line mesh-belt electric furnace (4.5 to 7.5 kW) placed right at the spring drop chute — springs drop directly onto belt. (Cost: ₹4.5L - ₹7.0L).
  • Alternative Batch Oven: Forced air circulation box tempering oven with digital PID controller for lower initial CapEx (₹1.2L - ₹2.0L).
